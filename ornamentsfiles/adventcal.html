Advent Calander

This layered laser-cut Advent calendar depicts the journey to Bethlehem, with numbered slots to count down the days to Christmas. Each slot holds a single, interchangeable figure, allowing you to choose one piece to place in all the slots. You can select between a praying angel, a flying angel, an angel with arms spread, or a wise man on a camel. The layered design creates depth, making this Advent calendar a beautiful and thoughtful way to celebrate the season while reflecting on the story of Jesus' birth.


Size:

12 x 12


Colors:

This ornament is tan, white and dark brown.
